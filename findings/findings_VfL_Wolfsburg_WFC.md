# Findings VfL Wolfsburg WFC

## Clustering results

For k = 2, Wolfsburg was safely clustered with front-footed teams. For k = 3, they were closely clustered with central combiners, before switching to crossers for k = 4 at a close margin.

### Like front-footed teams

Wolfsburg was less patient and slightly more direct in creating chances.

### Like central combiners

They had more touches in the final third centrally, while their central final third entries were more than average.

### Like wide crossers

They attempted more crosses, with more of their completed passes into the box being crosses as well. More of their final third touches and carries were more right.

## Pitch profile

Slightly more than a fourth of their attacks went centrally. They attacked slightly more on the right side than on the left.

## Formations and typical XI

Wolfsburg mostly rotated between 4-3-3 and 4-2-3-1, with 4-1-4-1 being the third option but by quite a margin. These three formations take up 97% of the usage.

For their main formation 4-3-3, striker, right winger, and right back were the only positions that saw significant rotation.

## Similar teams

Leicester City was the most similar team for squad and the second most for style. Manchester United was close on squad, while Inter was close on style.
