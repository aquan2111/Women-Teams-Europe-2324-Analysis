# Findings Aston Villa W

## Clustering results

For all of k = 2, k = 3, and k = 4, Aston Villa W were safely clustered with patient teams.

### Like its cluster

They stood out for less progressive passes per game, while less progression was done via passing. Their directness per shot-ending possession was less than average, while they were more patient in building up towards shots. They had less box entries via passing, they did not attempt through balls often, they were less aggressive with counterpress actions on opposition half, and they shot less off transitions. They passed into the final third centrally less.

### Unlike its cluster

Their defensive actions stayed at average height.

## Pitch profile

Most of their final third entries and touches were on wide areas, with roughly similar share between left and right flank. Only a fifth of their attacks went through the middle.

## Formations and typical XI

Aston Villa mainly split their time between 4-3-3 and 4-2-3-1 formations, while in 20% of the time they used back 3 formations and in 15% of the time they used wingerless formations, with 3-5-2 being the third most used formation.

For their main formation 4-3-3, right winger was the only position that saw significant rotation between Adriana Leon and Alisha Lehmann. For other positions, a player covered at least two thirds of the minutes.

## Similar teams

Atletico Madrid were the only team close to Aston Villa both on squad and style. Manchester United were the only FA Women's Super League teams appearing in top 5 of one of build, style, or both, in which Manchester United were the third closest on squad. The five closest teams on style to Aston Villa were all teams outside the FA Women's Super League.
