# Findings AS Roma W

## Clustering results

At k = 2, Roma was safely clustered with front-footed teams. Then at k = 3 and k = 4, Roma was safely clustered with wide crossers.

### Like its cluster

Roma was aggresive when it comes to their defending locations up the pitch and counterpress actions in opposition half. Their final third entries via passes into central areas were less than most wide crossers, while more final touches were on the right. They attempted a high proportion of their passes and completed passes into opposition box being crosses. Fewer of their shots come from delayed set piece, while fewer of their passes into the final third ended up centrally.

### Unlike its cluster

Roma was not as porgressive with their passes as most front-footed teams do, as more of their progression was done via carrying. They had slightly more touches in central areas in the final third. More of their box entries were carries than most wide crossers do.

## Pitch profile

Most of their final third entries and touches were on wide areas, particularly on the right. Both final third entries and touches on the left were more than centrally, although the gap was much smaller when it comes to touches.

## Formations and typical XI

Roma mostly used the 4-3-3 formation. They used back three formations 20% of the time and wingerless formations 19% of the time, with 3-5-2 being their second most used formation.

In their 4-3-3 formation, their goalkeeper of Ceasar, their center back pairing of Minami and Linari, their single pivot of Kumagai, and their striker of Giacinti were the more consistent positions. Di Guglielmo was a regular in the side, although she rotated between left back and right back. Giugliano was commonly used as one of the number 8 midfielders, usually more on the right. Haavi was mostly played as a left winger, although she was also a common option for right winger.

## Similar teams

Brighton was the closest team to Roma in terms of both squad and style, while Villarreal also appeared in top 5 for both these categories. The top 5 teams closest to Roma in terms of squad were also the top 5 teams closest to Roma overall.
